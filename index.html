Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

What materials are used in Utsaw Surya pressure cookers?

We use heavy-gauge 18/8 food-grade stainless steel with tri-ply technology (aluminum core sandwiched between steel layers) for optimal heat distribution and maximum longevity.

Do Utsaw Surya products work on Induction Cooktops?

Yes — all our tri-ply pressure cookers, stainless steel kadhais, and non-stick cookware are fully compatible with both Induction cooktops and traditional gas stoves.

What warranty is offered on products?

Our pressure cookers come with a 5-Year Manufacturer Warranty. Gas stoves and cookware sets come with a 2 to 3-Year warranty covering manufacturing defects.
