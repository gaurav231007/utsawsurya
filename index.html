Frequently Asked Questions

What materials are used?

We use heavy-gauge 18/8 food-grade stainless steel with tri-ply technology or hard-anodized options depending on the product.

Are the products induction compatible?

Yes — our tri-ply pressure cookers, stainless steel kadhais, and many cookware items are induction compatible. Check the product specifications for compatibility.

What warranty is offered?

Our pressure cookers typically have a 5-Year Manufacturer Warranty. Gas stoves and cookware sets carry 2-3 year warranties depending on the model.
